# FLTCOM v0.1.0-preview

This is the first public preview of FLTCOM, a fleet manager for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5. Its main purpose is to find out whether the new display works for you. Please tell us through the [UI feedback form](../../../../issues/new?template=ui-feedback.yml): which screen confused you, what felt slow, and how much you like the look.

## What it does

FLTCOM tracks every vessel in your save and shows the fleet on a 3D map in your browser. Pick a ship, compose an operation, read the briefing, and hold APPROVE. FLTCOM then flies the burns.

- **Fleet map and dossiers.** A 3D map of the Kerbol system with your ships, a fleet list, and a dossier per ship with orbit, resources, crew, and a stage blueprint.
- **Orbit changes.** Circularize, change apoapsis, periapsis or inclination, circular orbit at an altitude, standard low orbit, and raise apoapsis by an amount.
- **Launch to orbit.** From the launch pad to a target apoapsis, periapsis and inclination, optionally waiting for a plane.
- **Landings.** On the launch pad, the runway, or chosen coordinates, with a live descent readout.
- **Transfers.** To a moon or another planet, with a transfer window chart for planets.
- **Rendezvous and docking.** Meet another ship, then dock to a chosen port.
- **Satellite carriers.** Release the satellites on a carrier into an evenly spaced ring, equatorial or tilted, then bring the carrier home.
- **Groups.** Give several ships one order, or spread them evenly around one orbit. Ships far from the one you fly burn in the background.
- **Automatic systems.** Staging, landing gear, fairings, solar panels, antennas and radiators during an operation, all listed in the briefing.

Every operation gets a briefing first: burn timeline, delta-v budget per stage, contingencies and a plan preview. Red contingencies block approval.

## Install

1. Download the FLTCOM package from this release, not the source code archive.
2. Close KSP and back up your save.
3. Copy the package's `GameData/FLTCOM` folder into your KSP `GameData` folder. You should end up with `GameData/FLTCOM/FLTCOM.dll`.
4. Start KSP 1.12.5, load a save, and open `http://127.0.0.1:47800/` in a browser on the same computer, or click the FLTCOM toolbar button.

FLTCOM is not on CKAN yet.

## Known limits

- Constellation rings are equatorial or tilted, and only around the body the carrier orbits.
- Background burns only stage. Landing gear, fairings, panels, antennas and radiators work only on the ship you fly.
- A spent stage stays attached until you press space.
- The display is localhost only. A phone or another computer cannot connect.
- KSP 1.12.5 only.
- This is a preview. Back up your saves.
